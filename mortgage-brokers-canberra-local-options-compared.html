The clearest way to compare local options is to separate the route from the individual adviser. A first purchase, refinance, investment structure and construction drawdown each create a different paperwork trail, so the right lane depends on the job to be done. A borrower who already fits one bank's policy may prefer the direct lane; a borrower who needs several lenders weighed, or a scenario shaped before submission, is closer to the broker lane.

Start with three anchors: loan purpose, scheme position and timing. If ACT support could change cash at settlement, scheme checks belong beside rate comparison. If the build has staged builder invoices, drawdown alignment belongs beside lender fit. This framing also makes mortgage brokers canberra beginner guide explained useful background before shortlisting anyone.

  • First purchase: test deposit, duty concession eligibility and insurance triggers.
  • Refinance: measure the new position after exit costs, repayments, offsets and consolidation.
  • Investment: ask about interest-only structures, offset use and record keeping.
  • Construction: confirm progressive release matches builder payment stages.

Broker lane versus direct bank lane

The broker lane is built for breadth: the cited comparison point is 100+ lenders, including major banks, credit unions and specialist lenders, with no borrower fee in standard residential lending because commission is paid when a loan settles. The direct bank lane can be efficient when the applicant already meets that institution's credit policy and product range, but it narrows the field by design.

The trade-off is not secrecy versus access; it is fit versus speed. A single lender may move quickly for a clean scenario. A broader panel matters where income, contract type, guarantor position, equity, construction milestones or scheme timing need one structure tested against several policies. Ask for the shortlist logic, not just a logo list: why these lenders, which were excluded, and whether a specialist lender was genuinely relevant.

Fee clarity still belongs in the first meeting. Expect the Credit Guide before credit assistance, ask how commission is disclosed, and check whether any borrower-paid cost applies. ASIC Moneysmart's home loan material gives an independent baseline for repayments, interest rates and costs.

ACT numbers that must survive verification

Canberra comparisons often hinge on scheme figures, so keep them precise and provisional. For eligible buyers, from July 2025, the ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme is described as no stamp duty up to $1,020,000, concessional rates up to $1,455,000 and a maximum saving of $35,238. The stated conditions are no Australian property ownership in the past 5 years and at least 1 year living in the property.

Nationally, from October 2025, the First Home Guarantee is described as allowing a 5% deposit without lenders mortgage insurance, while Help to Buy may allow a 2% deposit. None of these figures settles eligibility by itself. The practical test is whether the buyer qualifies, the lender accepts the structure, and settlement timing still works when those moving parts are combined.

  • Duty: confirm the price cap and concession tier against the contract.
  • LMI: ask when insurance applies and whether a guarantee changes it.
  • Evidence: identify proof of occupancy, prior ownership and deposit source early.
  • Timing: do not rely on pre-approval until conditions are known.

Questions that reveal real local fit

Rather than asking who is biggest, ask which problems each option solves. A strong broker conversation should reduce uncertainty before a credit application is lodged: which lenders are in scope, whether specialist lenders matter, what documents are missing, and whether the adviser operates under an Australian Credit Licence or as an authorised credit representative.

For refinancers, the sharper question is net position after exit costs rather than headline rate alone. For investors, the sharper question is structure: interest-only options, offset accounts and how debt will be explained at tax time. For construction borrowers, the sharper question is control: how each progressive drawdown is released and what happens if a stage is delayed.
